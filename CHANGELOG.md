# Changelog

What each release brought, in short. Its notes on [GitHub](https://github.com/DariusHQ/fiverunner/releases) tell all of it.

## [1.5.4](https://github.com/DariusHQ/fiverunner/releases/tag/v1.5.4) - 2026-11-08

Under the screen of each player, the dev UI and the playground show the keys that resources bound with `RegisterKeyMapping`, with what they do, and a click presses one. The test that `fiverunner init` writes for a server fails when one of its resources does not start, as when it needs a resource that the server lacks, which printed no script error. Links of the playground load more of what popular scripts need: jim_bridge for the scripts of jimathy, screencapture for screenshot-basic, as npwd needs, pma-voice, ps_lib, it_bridge, httpmanager, duff and iblips. 46 of the 69 most starred FiveM repositories that the playground lays out start all of their resources, where 40 did.

## [1.5.3](https://github.com/DariusHQ/fiverunner/releases/tag/v1.5.3) - 2026-11-04

The maps of the dev UI and of the playground, and the worlds behind the screens of players, start as the cartoon, as the README and the demo show them. Players of ESX join without warnings of natives: what peds wear streams in at once, and the HUD, the traffic, the look of players, the pattern of plates and the list of the models of GTA V do nothing quietly.

## [1.5.2](https://github.com/DariusHQ/fiverunner/releases/tag/v1.5.2) - 2026-11-04

Servers with hundreds of convars, as the recipes of txAdmin set, start faster: a test of the Stable recipe of Qbox starts 20% sooner. `fiverunner mcp` names Codex among the assistants that it serves, and [docs/mcp.md](docs/mcp.md) sets it up in Codex, with the time that its first start, big servers and audits need.

## [1.5.1](https://github.com/DariusHQ/fiverunner/releases/tag/v1.5.1) - 2026-11-04

The characters of the cartoon draw their heads whole whichever way they face: those that faced left had a hole in their hair and a dark band by their ear, and long hair, which looked like headphones, falls behind their heads. The demo and the pictures of the README are recorded again with them.

## [1.5.0](https://github.com/DariusHQ/fiverunner/releases/tag/v1.5.0) - 2026-11-04

The tests that `fiverunner init` writes log a player in with a character on servers of Qbox, QBCore and ESX, and fail on the scripts that break as it loads. Links of the playground spawn the players of ESX, give Qbox the table of skins that its character menu reads, bring each repository of `&with=`, and ask GitHub for the tree of a repository once. The audit counts what an event gives after it waited for that event. The repository tells how to report vulnerabilities, answers what people ask first, and has a form for scripts that do not run.

## [1.4.0](https://github.com/DariusHQ/fiverunner/releases/tag/v1.4.0) - 2026-11-04

`fiverunner mcp` serves the Model Context Protocol, so AI assistants such as Claude Code, Cursor and VS Code run your FiveM project in FiveRunner while they work on it: they describe what its server started, run its tests, run Lua against a fresh server of it with players that join and trigger events, and audit its events.

## [1.3.0](https://github.com/DariusHQ/fiverunner/releases/tag/v1.3.0) - 2026-11-04

`fiverunner audit` triggers the events of a server that clients may trigger, as cheaters do, with a character logged in for Qbox, QBCore and ESX, and tells what any client gains, such as money, items or a job, what it changes in the database, and what breaks, exiting with 1 for CI when a client gains anything. Tests list the events that resources handle and read output from a line on, and the guide of tests shows how players log in to frameworks. Links of the playground tell their visitors to connect a player.

## [1.2.2](https://github.com/DariusHQ/fiverunner/releases/tag/v1.2.2) - 2026-11-04

Players of Qbox join without a warning for each of its 901 vehicles, as FiveRunner names vehicle models by their hashes, which Qbox gives labels to. Links of the playground run ESX with the tables of its recipe, give QBCore, Qbox, ESX and ox_lib the commands that their recipes give them, keep the link when the forms under its card are used, and tell why reading a repository failed.

## [1.2.1](https://github.com/DariusHQ/fiverunner/releases/tag/v1.2.1) - 2026-11-03

Links of the playground run more of the popular scripts of GitHub: those that use the exports of QBCore, Qbox, ESX, ox_target or qb-target without their manifests naming them get them, and ox_inventory alone runs with Qbox. Laid out as their links lay them out, 5 more of the 80 most starred FiveM repositories pass the test that `fiverunner init` writes.

## [1.2.0](https://github.com/DariusHQ/fiverunner/releases/tag/v1.2.0) - 2026-11-03

The playground runs repositories of GitHub from links, `?gh=owner/repo`, with the frameworks that they need, such as ox_lib, QBCore, Qbox and ESX, and gives the button of a README that opens them. `fiverunner init` writes a workflow that runs the new [action](https://github.com/DariusHQ/fiverunner-action) of FiveRunner. Players of the dev UI reach servers once their databases are open.

## [1.1.1](https://github.com/DariusHQ/fiverunner/releases/tag/v1.1.1) - 2026-11-02

The first minutes of new users. The test that `fiverunner init` writes fails on scripts that do not load and on client scripts, and passes on QBCore, Qbox and ESX as they come. Players of tests get a license, as those of the dev UI do. Scripts under asset escrow are skipped with a clear word, and the dev UI and the playground fit on phones.

## [1.1.0](https://github.com/DariusHQ/fiverunner/releases/tag/v1.1.0) - 2026-11-01

Tests report to the tools around them: coverage of Lua for lcov, JUnit for CI, and types for the Lua language server of editors. The inspector lists the keys that resources bind.

## [1.0.0](https://github.com/DariusHQ/fiverunner/releases/tag/v1.0.0) - 2026-11-01

A [playground](https://dariushq.github.io/fiverunner/) that runs in the page, with nothing to install, and a small city to play in it, in Lua and JavaScript. The adaptive cards of deferrals are forms, which tests answer too.

## [0.12.0](https://github.com/DariusHQ/fiverunner/releases/tag/v0.12.0) - 2026-11-01

The dev UI gets an inspector of the server and of each player: what each resource has, the time it takes each tick, the convars, and a box that runs code inside a resource.

## [0.11.0](https://github.com/DariusHQ/fiverunner/releases/tag/v0.11.0) - 2026-11-01

Resources in JavaScript run, on QuickJS, in the browser and in CI, with what they ask of Node. The phone of npwd works in the Stable recipe of Qbox, and the drivers of MySQL that resources carry reach FiveRunner's database.

## [0.10.0](https://github.com/DariusHQ/fiverunner/releases/tag/v0.10.0) - 2026-10-31

The whole recipe of QBCore runs, 72 resources, and CI plays it. Players walk and drive with W, A, S and D on their screens. Vehicles keep their colours, mods and health, so they come out of garages as they went in. A contributing guide, this changelog and forms for issues.

## [0.9.0](https://github.com/DariusHQ/fiverunner/releases/tag/v0.9.0) - 2026-10-31

The first version on npm. The big NUIs of Qbox's Stable recipe work as players use them: illenium-appearance, ox_inventory and Renewed-Banking. The screen of each player shows the world round their ped, and the demo is a film of 2 minutes, with sound.

## [0.8.0](https://github.com/DariusHQ/fiverunner/releases/tag/v0.8.0) - 2026-10-29

The map of the dev UI gets two new styles: a cartoon of little characters, and the pause menu of GTA V.

## [0.7.0](https://github.com/DariusHQ/fiverunner/releases/tag/v0.7.0) - 2026-10-28

The Stable recipe of Qbox gets nearly every native that it calls: cameras, doors, interiors, appearance, weapons, death and seats. CI runs the recipe on every change.

## [0.6.1](https://github.com/DariusHQ/fiverunner/releases/tag/v0.6.1) - 2026-10-27

Players dropped on blips stand where the blips are, and a new demo shows a player who takes a car out of a garage.

## [0.6.0](https://github.com/DariusHQ/fiverunner/releases/tag/v0.6.0) - 2026-10-27

The whole Stable recipe of Qbox runs, 43 resources in about 6 seconds. The dev UI gets a map of the world, and players a keyboard.

## [0.5.2](https://github.com/DariusHQ/fiverunner/releases/tag/v0.5.2) - 2026-10-22

Entities get health and armour, and the first demo shows the tests of QBox and a player who registers a character.

## [0.5.1](https://github.com/DariusHQ/fiverunner/releases/tag/v0.5.1) - 2026-10-22

Players play through QBox in the dev UI, from its character menu to the database.

## [0.5.0](https://github.com/DariusHQ/fiverunner/releases/tag/v0.5.0) - 2026-10-22

`fiverunner init`, `fiverunner test --watch`, mocks of natives and tests of NUI. The dev UI restarts only the resources that you change.

## [0.4.0](https://github.com/DariusHQ/fiverunner/releases/tag/v0.4.0) - 2026-10-22

QBox and ESX run, with oxmysql's exports on a database in memory.

## [0.3.0](https://github.com/DariusHQ/fiverunner/releases/tag/v0.3.0) - 2026-10-21

## [0.2.0](https://github.com/DariusHQ/fiverunner/releases/tag/v0.2.0) - 2026-10-15

`fiverunner test` runs the Lua tests of resources, on your machine or in GitHub Actions.

## [0.1.0](https://github.com/DariusHQ/fiverunner/releases/tag/v0.1.0) - 2026-10-15

The engine: everything that runs FiveM resources on a server and its clients, in Node or in a browser.
